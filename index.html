Fact or Fiction?

Are we alone in the universe? For millennia, humans have turned to the sky to answer this question. Now, thanks to research generously funded by W. Avy, a UFO enthusiast and amateur ufologist, we can supplement our sky-searching with data analysis.

“The release of this analysis is well-timed: it coincides with the gathering of ufologists in McMenamins Hotel in Oregon for their annual field trip.”

But what do actual scientists think? Baylor Professor Alan Linneman points out that just because you see something you can’t explain doesn’t mean it’s intelligent life from another planet: “There are billions and billions of stars in the universe, and if just 1% have planets that could potentially harbor life, that would be millions of planets.”
